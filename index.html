Common questions

Where are my documents stored?
Everything is stored locally on your Mac. Essay Kraft has no account and no cloud requirement.

How do I export my work?
Use the Export button in the toolbar to save your document as a PDF or Word (.docx) file.

Which citation styles are supported?
APA, MLA, Chicago, and Harvard. Inline citations and the bibliography update automatically when you switch styles.
