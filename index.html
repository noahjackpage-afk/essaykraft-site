Questions

Where are my documents stored?

On your Mac or iPad. There’s no account and no cloud requirement.

Which formats can I export?

PDF, Word (.docx) and RTF. You can also print, and import .docx and .rtf files.

Does it need the internet?

Only for Find Online, which looks up a source by title, author, DOI or ISBN. Everything else works offline.

Can I bring my references with me?

Yes. Import BibTeX or RIS from Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote or journal sites, and export them back out.
