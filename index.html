Abstract

EssayKraft is a writing app for students. It gives you real pages, formats your citations as you write, and keeps a private record that proves the work is yours. Everything else stays out of the way.

Keywords: essays, citations, authorship, Mac, iPad

Appendix A.
Questions

A.1Where are my documents stored?

On your Mac or iPad. There’s no account and no cloud requirement.

A.2Which formats can I export?

PDF, Word (.docx) and RTF. You can also print, and import .docx and .rtf files.

A.3Does it need the internet?

Only for Find Online. Everything else works offline.

A.4Is there a subscription?

No. One purchase and it’s yours.
