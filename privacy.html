Privacy Policy

Essay Kraft. Last updated 16 July 2026.

Essay Kraft is designed to keep your work private. Your documents, references, and settings are stored locally on your device. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking, and it never uploads your documents to any server.

Reference lookup

Essay Kraft includes an optional reference lookup feature. When, and only when, you actively search for a reference, the search terms you enter (such as a title, author, DOI, or ISBN) are sent over a secure connection to third-party academic databases in order to return matching citation details:

  • Crossref (see the Crossref privacy notice)
  • Open Library, from the Internet Archive (see the Internet Archive privacy policy)

Only the text of your search is sent. Your documents, personal identity, and account information are never transmitted, and search terms are not stored by Essay Kraft or linked to you. If you never use the reference lookup feature, Essay Kraft makes no network requests at all.
